FAIR HOUSING AND RENEWAL POLICY GUIDELINES

1. PROTECTED CLASSES
Under the Fair Housing Act, renewal decisions and pricing must not be based on or correlated with: race, color, national origin, religion, sex, familial status, or disability. State and local laws may add protections such as source of income, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, and marital status.

2. CONSISTENT TREATMENT
All residents in similar circumstances must be evaluated using the same criteria. Rent increases, renewal offers, and non-renewals must be based on objective factors such as payment history, lease compliance, market rate, and property-level pricing strategy.

3. RENT INCREASE LIMITS
Any rent increase greater than 10% must be flagged for human review. Increases above 15% require documented justification tied to market rate and property performance, not individual resident characteristics.

4. NON-RENEWAL CRITERIA
A non-renewal recommendation is permitted only when supported by documented, objective reasons: repeated missed payments (2 or more in the lease term), serious lease violations, or property-wide strategic decisions. Non-renewals must always be escalated to human review before any communication is sent.

5. RETENTION DISCOUNTS
Retention discounts may be offered to long-tenured residents (24+ months) with strong payment history. Discount eligibility must follow consistent criteria, not subjective judgment about individual residents.

6. AUDIT TRAIL REQUIREMENT
Every renewal decision must include a documented reasoning trace: the inputs considered, the rules applied, and the final recommendation. All AI-generated decisions must be reviewable by a human and stored for compliance audit.

7. HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP TRIGGERS
The following decisions must be escalated to human review before action:
- Any non-renewal recommendation
- Rent increases above 10%
- Residents with active maintenance disputes
- Residents with fewer than 6 months of payment history
- Any case where the Compliance Agent flags potential fair housing risk

8. COMMUNICATION STANDARDS
Renewal communications must be respectful, clear, and consistent in tone regardless of resident characteristics. All communications must include the resident's right to discuss the terms with management.
